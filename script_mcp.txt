Your AI can't use your apps on its own. MCP is the plug that lets it. Think of USB-C. One shape of plug, and any device works with any charger. MCP does that for AI. It's a shared standard, so an AI app can connect to tools like your calendar, your files, or a database. A tool shares a menu of what it can do. The AI reads the menu, picks an action, and asks for it. The tool does the job and sends back the result. Before this, every connection had to be custom built. Now one tool can work with many AI apps. The catch: a connected tool can act for you, so only plug in ones you trust. Next up: what is an encryption key?

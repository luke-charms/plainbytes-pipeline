Some AI text carries a hidden signature that you can't see. Think of a chef who adds the same tiny pinch of salt to every dish, too small to taste, but a lab test can find it. When an AI writes, it picks each next word from a list of good options. A secret key quietly nudges those picks toward certain words, and over a whole paragraph, the nudges form a pattern. A detector with the same key checks for that pattern, and a match means the text probably came from that AI. The catch: short texts, heavy rewriting, or translation can wash the signature out, and it only works for models that add one. Next up: how HTTPS keeps your data private.

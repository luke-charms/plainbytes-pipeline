Why does a video load instantly, even when the website is on the other side of the planet? Because it usually isn't coming from there. Think of a C D N as a chain of corner shops. Instead of one giant warehouse far away, copies of popular files are stored on servers in cities around the world. When you open a page, you get the files from the nearest one. A shorter trip means faster loading, and less traffic for the main server. Streaming sites, shops and news pages all use one.

===== DESCRIPTION =====
Why does a shop remember your cart, even after you close the tab?
A cookie is a tiny file your browser stores with a random number on it. Next visit, your browser hands it back, so the site knows it's you. Some cookies come from advertisers and follow you across sites, which is why browsers let you block them.

Bluetooth isn't one steady signal. Here's what's actually happening. Think of two friends whispering across a noisy party, changing spots every second so nobody can drown them out. Your phone and your earbuds share a secret pattern. Many times a second, they jump together between dozens of radio channels. That keeps them clear of Wi-Fi and everyone else's gadgets. Your phone chops the music into tiny packets, and the earbuds put them back in order. It uses very little power, so a small battery lasts for hours.

Wi-Fi isn't magic, and it never touches the internet itself. So what is it actually doing? Think of your router as a radio station. Your phone sends data as invisible radio waves, and the router picks them up and passes them along a cable to the internet. Every message is chopped into tiny packets, and each one is tagged with an address for the device. That way many devices can share the same air without mixing up their mail.

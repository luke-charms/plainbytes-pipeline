A passkey is a login you never have to type, or remember. Think of a lock the website installs, and a key that stays safely on your devices. When you sign up, your phone makes two matching keys. The website keeps the public one, and your phone keeps the private one. To log in, the site sends a challenge, and your phone signs it after a fingerprint or face check. The site checks the signature, and you're in. Because no secret is ever sent, fake sites can't steal it. The catch: if you lose your devices and have no backup, getting back in gets harder.

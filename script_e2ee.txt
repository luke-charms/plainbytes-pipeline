End-to-end encryption means even the chat app can't read your messages. Here's what's actually happening. Think of a padlock. Your friend hands out open padlocks, but keeps the only key. Your phone locks each message with that padlock before it leaves. The company's servers just pass along a scrambled box they can't open. Only your friend's phone has the key, so only it can unlock the message. The keys are made on the devices and never leave them. The catch: it protects messages on the way, not on the screen.

Wi-Fi isn't magic, and it never touches the internet itself. So what is it actually doing?
Your phone sends data as invisible radio waves, the router catches them and passes them down a cable to the internet. Every message is split into tiny labelled packets, so many devices can share the same air without mixing up their mail.

Your computer has a memory that wipes itself every time you shut it down. It's called RAM. Think of RAM as your desk, and your storage as a filing cabinet. The cabinet holds everything, but it's slow to reach into. So the computer pulls what you're using onto the desk, where the processor can grab it almost instantly. A bigger desk means more things open at once, without constant shuffling. Inside, RAM holds tiny electric charges that leak away, so it has to refresh them over and over, every second.

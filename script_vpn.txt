A VPN doesn't make you invisible online, it just changes who can see you. Think of sending your mail through a private courier, instead of the local post office. Without a VPN, your internet provider sees every site you visit. With one, your phone locks your traffic and sends it to the VPN server first. Your provider only sees scrambled data going to one place. Then the VPN server visits the website for you, so the site sees the server's address, not yours. The catch: the VPN company can now see your activity, and it won't stop logins, cookies, or viruses from tracking you. Next up: what a passkey is. Follow PlainBytes for more tech, explained simply.

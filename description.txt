A VPN doesn't make you invisible online. It just changes who can see you.
Without one, your internet provider sees every site you visit. With one, your traffic is locked and sent to the VPN server first, so the website sees the server's address, not yours. The catch: the VPN company can see your activity, and logins, cookies and viruses can still track you.

A Bluetooth tracker has no GPS and no internet, so how does it find your lost keys? It borrows the phones of strangers. Imagine shouting I am here in a crowded street. You can't call anyone, but people walking past hear you. A tracker does the same, sending a tiny Bluetooth signal every few seconds. Any phone in the network that passes by hears it, notes where it is, and quietly sends that spot online. The report is encrypted, so only you can read it, and the phone that passed by cannot read it either. Millions of phones turn into one giant search party.

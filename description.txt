An AI agent isn't just a chatbot. It actually does things for you.
An agent is a language model that makes a plan, picks a tool like a browser or calendar, tries a step, looks at the result and repeats until the job is done. The catch: it can make mistakes while acting, which is why limits and kill switches matter.

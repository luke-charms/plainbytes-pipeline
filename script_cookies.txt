Why does a shop remember your cart, even after you close the tab? Because of a tiny file called a cookie. Think of it as a cloakroom ticket. The first time you visit, the website hands your browser a small note with a random number on it. Your browser keeps it. Next visit, it hands the note back, and the site says: ah, you again, here is your cart. Cookies also keep you logged in, so you don't type your password on every page. The catch: some cookies come from advertisers, and they follow you from site to site to learn what you like. That's why browsers now let you block them. Next up: how two factor authentication works.

The cloud isn't in the sky. It's a giant warehouse full of computers.
When you save a photo to the cloud, it's stored on another company's hard drives inside a data center, with copies on several machines so one broken drive loses nothing. The catch: it's still another company's computer, and data centers use huge amounts of electricity.

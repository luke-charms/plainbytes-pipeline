Your data hops across many machines online, and nobody on the way can read it. Here's how HTTPS works in 35 seconds.

HTTPS first checks the website's ID (a certificate), then your browser and the site agree on a secret key using clever math, and everything is scrambled with it. It hides what you send, not always which site you visit, and a padlock doesn't prove a site is trustworthy.

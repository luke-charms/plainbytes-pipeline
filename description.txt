Your OLED screen has no backlight at all. Here's why that matters.

An LCD uses an always-on lamp with tiny shutters in front. An OLED makes its own light in every pixel, so a black pixel simply switches off. The catch: the organic materials wear out over time, which is where burn-in comes from.

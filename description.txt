Your AI can't use your apps on its own. MCP is the plug that lets it.
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a shared standard that lets AI apps connect to tools like calendars, files and databases. A tool shares a menu of what it can do, the AI picks an action, and the tool sends back the result.

One fake message can steal your password without any hacking. Here's how phishing really works.
Phishing is a scam message that looks like your bank, a delivery or a sale, and sends you to a fake copy of a real website. You type your password, and the scammer gets it. Check the address, type it yourself, or use a passkey.

A passkey is a login you never have to type or remember.
When you sign up, your phone makes two matching keys: the website keeps the public one and your phone keeps the private one. To log in, the site sends a challenge and your phone signs it after a fingerprint or face check, so no secret is ever sent. The catch: if you lose all your devices and have no backup, getting back in gets harder.

Why can a hacker know your password and still not get into your account? Because of two factor authentication. Think of a front door with two locks. The first lock is your password. The second lock is something only you have, like your phone. When you log in, the site asks for a six digit code. Your phone app makes that code from a secret key and the current time, and it changes every thirty seconds. A thief with only your password has no code, so the door stays shut. The catch: codes sent by text message can be stolen by tricking your phone company, so an authenticator app is safer. Next up: what a firewall does.

How can one fake message steal your password without any hacking? It's called phishing, and it works just like fishing. The bait is a message that looks real, like your bank, a delivery, or a Black Friday deal. It says hurry, or your account will be locked. You tap the link and land on a copy of the real website. You type your password, and the scammer receives it instantly. Nothing was hacked. You simply handed it over. The catch: the web address is often one letter off, like a number one instead of an L.

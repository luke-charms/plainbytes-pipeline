The cloud isn't in the sky. It's a giant warehouse full of computers. Save a photo to the cloud, and it's stored on another company's hard drives, inside a building called a data center. Think of renting a storage unit instead of buying a bigger house. Your phone sends the photo over the internet, and the data center keeps copies on several machines, so one broken drive loses nothing. Later, from any device, you log in and your photo is fetched right back. Companies rent the same computers by the hour, so they never have to buy their own.

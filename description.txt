Your computer's memory wipes itself every time you shut it down. Here's why.
RAM is your computer's desk: a fast workspace where it keeps what you're using right now, while storage is the slow filing cabinet. RAM holds tiny electric charges that leak away, so it constantly refreshes them, and when the power goes, the desk wipes clean.

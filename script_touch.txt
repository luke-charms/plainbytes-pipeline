Your screen doesn't feel pressure. It feels you. Think of a net of invisible tripwires under the glass, a grid of tiny electrodes, each holding a small electric charge. Your finger is slightly conductive, so when it gets close, it steals a little of that charge. The phone checks every crossing of the grid, finds where the charge dropped most, and that spot is your touch. It repeats this about a hundred times a second. Two fingers just means two dips, which is how pinch to zoom works. The catch: normal gloves and plastic pens do nothing, because they don't conduct. Next up: how Bluetooth works.

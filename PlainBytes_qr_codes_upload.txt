===== DESCRIPTION =====
A QR code is just a picture. So how does your phone read it?

It's a tiny map of black and white squares: each one is a 1 or a 0, and together they spell out a web address. Three corner markers tell your camera which way is up, and built-in backup data keeps scratched codes working. The catch: a QR code can hide any link, so check before you tap.
